If you're in crisis

If you're in immediate distress, please reach out. In the United States, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). In the UK, call 116 123 (Samaritans). Outside those, your local emergency number or findahelpline.com can help.

DiaryWell is a self-tracking tool, not a substitute for medical care. Always talk with your clinician about treatment decisions.
